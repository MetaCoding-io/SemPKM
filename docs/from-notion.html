Sound Familiar?

The problems Notion power users won't talk about

Your data lives on Notion's servers. You can export it, but what you get back is a pile of Markdown files and CSV tables that lose structure, relations, and views. You don't own your data — you rent it.
Performance cliffs are real. A workspace with a few hundred pages feels snappy. Grow that to thousands and everything slows — search lags, databases take seconds to load, and the UI stutters.
Relations are flat and fragile. Notion lets you link databases, but every relation is the same — untyped, undirected, and invisible in the API export. No way to say how two things connect.
Properties are optional by default. Nothing stops you from leaving a required field blank, choosing the wrong type, or entering inconsistent data. Your "database" is really a spreadsheet wearing a disguise.
How SemPKM Solves This

Same paradigm, no lock-in

Tables, cards, dashboards, and forms — everything you use in Notion, rebuilt to enforce structure and run on your machine.

Import Your Workspace

Upload your Notion export ZIP. A guided wizard maps your databases to types, columns to properties, and relations to typed edges.

Table & Card Views

Sort, filter, and group your objects just like Notion database views. Switch between table and card layouts with a click.

Dashboards

Build multi-view dashboards with CSS Grid layouts. Tables, cards, and charts in one screen with cross-view filtering — like Notion dashboards, but more flexible.

Create Types Like Databases

In-app type creation works like "create a database" in Notion. Name it, pick an icon, add properties — but every property has a real type and validation rules.

Forms with Real Validation

Properties are enforced, not optional. Leave a required field blank and the form tells you. Enter the wrong type and it catches it. Your data stays consistent.

Self-Hosted

Your data lives on your machine, in a format you control. Export anytime. No subscription required, no servers to trust, no vendor that can change the terms.

Full History & Undo

Every change is an immutable event. See who changed what, when, and why. Undo anything at any time — something Notion simply can't offer.
