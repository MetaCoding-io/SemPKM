# SemPKM Marketing Research

This directory contains evidence-backed marketing and positioning research for SemPKM.

## Contents

- `pkm-complaint-corpus.csv` — coded corpus of high-signal public discussions about structured PKM pain points. This is intentionally a working research dataset, not a polished narrative. Target size: 50–75 discussions.
- `product-marketing-principles.md` — durable product/marketing principles that have emerged repeatedly from the research.
- `jobs-pains-gains-positioning.md` — Jobs/Pains/Gains profile and positioning matrix for the initial beachhead: structured-PKM system builders, especially schema-strained Obsidian users.

## Research status

The corpus is not yet at the intended 50–75 discussion target. Earlier research established the taxonomy and produced a substantial high-signal qualitative sample, but did **not** complete the promised quantitative-ish corpus. The current dataset should therefore be treated as v0.1: sufficient to support a first positioning synthesis, but not yet complete enough to claim prevalence estimates.

The corpus should be extended by coding additional discussions against the same taxonomy rather than adding new categories opportunistically.
